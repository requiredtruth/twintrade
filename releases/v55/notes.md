Adds a persistent Sound level control with Soft (45%), Balanced (70%) and Full (100%) choices. Full remains the backward-compatible default; invalid saved values safely recover to Full.

The level scales event phrases, directional ticks, candle cues, crossings, previews and liquidations consistently across standalone WebAudio, APK foreground native PCM and Android background monitoring. Existing system media volume, mute, audio focus, Gentle/Warm/Minimal profiles, event-priority arbitration and reversal hysteresis are preserved.

Validated with the complete JavaScript/native regression suite, mobile browser checks, Android compilation, signature/alignment/package checks and Android 15 lifecycle/audio-focus instrumentation. No funded transaction or physical-device listening test was performed.

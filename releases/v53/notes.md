Adds a compact Event sound character setting with Gentle, Warm and Minimal choices. Gentle keeps the detailed three-note phrases, Warm lowers and softens them, and Minimal uses a quieter two-note phrase.

The choice persists locally and is carried through the existing private native handoff, keeping foreground and Android background public-execution sounds consistent. Price/candle sonification, mute controls, audio focus, holding/funding behavior and trading controls remain unchanged.

Validated with 25 JavaScript suites, ten native suites, browser audio/layout checks, Android compilation, signature/alignment/package checks and Android 15 lifecycle/audio-focus instrumentation. No funded transaction or physical-device listening test was performed.

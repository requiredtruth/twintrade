Trading profile 
Cumulative net P&L
Closed trades across all markets in this mode. Fees and holding costs are included in recorded net P&L. This device retains up to 200 closed trades; this is not guaranteed lifetime history. Missing settlements are excluded. Trades with unknown collateral still contribute dollar P&L, but are excluded from return ratios.
How the statistics work
Sharpe = mean per-trade return ÷ sample standard deviation. Sortino = mean per-trade return ÷ downside deviation (root mean square of negative returns, including zero for nonnegative trades). Return = net P&L ÷ that trade’s collateral. Both use a zero target, are not annualized, and require at least two valid trades and nonzero deviation. Overlapping trades are separate observations. Drawdown is the largest dollar decline from a previous peak in cumulative closed-trade P&L, not intratrade or account-equity drawdown. Profit factor is gross winning net P&L ÷ absolute losing net P&L; it is unavailable with no losing trades.
